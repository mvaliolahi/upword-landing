Why Upword?

Everything for real language learning

Upword isn't just an educational app — it's your complete companion on the language-learning journey. From your first word to fluent conversation, all in one place.

Real AI Conversation

Chat with a smart assistant that adapts to your level. Everyday topics like travel, restaurants, work, and health — without fear of making mistakes.

Interactive Missions

Role-play in real scenarios: job interviews, hotel bookings, doctor visits. Each mission is a step toward speaking more fluently.

Shadowing Practice

Listen and repeat. Refine your pronunciation with targeted exercises and build the confidence to actually speak.

Smart Vocabulary

Learn new words in context, not as a dry list. Each word sticks with real examples and native pronunciation.

XP & Streak System

Earn XP with every exercise, keep your daily streak alive, and level up. Learning becomes a rewarding habit.

Personalized Path

From complete beginner to advanced, the learning path is tuned to your progress. Every user gets a unique experience.

FAQ

Everything you want to know

Is Upword free? +
Downloading and starting Upword is free. You can try daily conversations, missions, and shadowing practice. For access to advanced content, special missions, and more languages, you can make in-app purchases.
Do I need internet to use it? +
AI conversations require internet. However, you can review learned vocabulary and shadowing exercises offline.
Which languages are supported? +
Currently English, Turkish, Arabic, German, French, and Spanish are supported. More languages are on the way.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. All your learning data is stored locally on your device. Conversations are only sent to the AI service for processing and are not retained.
What level do I need? +
No specific level needed. Upword supports complete beginners to advanced learners. At the start, your level is detected and your path is personalized.
